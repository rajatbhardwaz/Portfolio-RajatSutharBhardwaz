02 / HOW I THINK

Clarity is a
build material.

Useful work starts by understanding what is actually at stake. From there, I connect the user’s experience with the details that make a product viable.

  1. 01

    Understand

    Identify the need, context, constraints, and the question beneath the brief.

  2. 02

    Explore

    Look for useful directions before committing to the most obvious answer.

  3. 03

    Design

    Give the idea shape through systems, interfaces, and deliberate decisions.

  4. 04

    Build

    Turn the direction into a working, resilient, and useful experience.

  5. 05

    Refine

    Test what matters, remove friction, and make the last details earn their place.

04 / THE CASE-STUDY LENS

A polished outcome
is only half the story.

When work is ready to be published, the useful evidence is not just its final surface. It is the reasoning that made the surface make sense.

01

The opportunity

What needed to become clearer, simpler, or more possible?

02

The people

Who would use it, and what did they need from the experience?

03

The decision

Which trade-offs shaped the route forward, and why?

04

The making

How did interface, system, and implementation come together?

05

The learning

What did the work reveal that should change the next iteration?
